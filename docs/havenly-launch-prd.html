Summary

Havenly is a community app for university students in Dubai: a feed of places and events picked by students, interest and campus communities, messaging, a live map and brand perks. On top of that sits a housing section where students find student residences near their university and send an enquiry. Residences pay Havenly a referral fee for every student who signs. Students pay nothing.

The social side brings students in and keeps them coming back. Housing is how Havenly earns money. This document covers what has to be true for the first public launch, what's already built, and what's left.

Problem

Dubai's student population is mostly expats and new arrivals. They land with no local network, no feel for which areas are close to campus, and no trusted way to find somewhere to live. Housing search happens across agency listings, social media groups and word of mouth, where prices are unclear and it's hard to tell which places actually take students.

Student residences have the opposite problem: they need a steady flow of students who are ready to move in, at the start of each term, and today they reach them through agents and ads with no guarantee of a signed contract.

If we don't solve it: students keep finding housing through scattered, unverified channels, and Havenly is a pleasant social app with no revenue.

Who it's for

New studentJust arrived or arriving next term. Needs friends, places to go and somewhere to live near campus. Price-sensitive and moves around term dates.
Returning studentAlready settled. Uses the feed, communities and perks; may switch residence between years.
Partner residenceA student residence operator or licensed agency. Wants qualified enquiries, booked viewings and signed students, and only wants to pay for results.
Havenly teamAdds listings, passes enquiries to residences, tracks each deal through to a signed contract, and invoices the referral fee.

Brands offering perks are a secondary audience and are not the focus of this release.

Goals

For students

  • A new student can find residences near their university, see real room prices, and send an enquiry in under 2 minutes.
  • Every enquiry gets a response from the residence within 24 hours.
  • Students feel part of a community in their first weeks, through campus and interest communities.

For the business

  • Sign [N] founding residences before launch, each listed with photos, room prices and map location.
  • Turn enquiries into signed students, and invoice the referral fee on every signed deal.
  • Grow a student audience large enough that housing enquiries come from inside the app, not paid ads.

Non-goals for v1

  • No in-app contracts, deposits or rent payments. Handling money or signing tenancies would make Havenly a party to the deal and add regulatory and refund risk. Residences contract directly with students.
  • No self-listing by residences. The Havenly team adds every listing, which keeps quality high while there are only a handful of partners.
  • No roommate matching. Useful later, once there are enough listings and students to match.
  • No other cities or emirates. Dubai only, so the team can know every residence personally.
  • No automated fee invoicing. Fees are tracked in the app and invoiced manually until deal volume justifies building it.

User stories

New student

  • As a new student, I want to see residences close to my university so that I don't waste time on places with a long commute.
  • As a new student, I want to see real room prices per year, semester or month so that I can tell what fits my budget before I contact anyone.
  • As a new student, I want to ask a question or book a viewing in one form so that I don't have to chase residences on WhatsApp.
  • As a new student, I want to know when a residence has replied or booked my viewing so that I'm not left wondering.
  • As a new student, I want to save places to compare later so that I can decide with my family.
  • As a new student, I want to know Havenly is free for me and that I sign directly with the residence so that I trust the process.

Partner residence

  • As a residence, I want each enquiry to include move-in month, length of stay, room, budget and a phone number so that my team can follow up without back-and-forth.
  • As a residence, I want to pay only for students who sign so that listing on Havenly carries no upfront risk.
  • As a residence, I want to appear for the universities I'm actually close to so that I reach students who will realistically live with me.

Havenly team

  • As a team member, I want to be alerted the moment an enquiry comes in so that no lead waits overnight.
  • As a team member, I want to move each enquiry through contacted, viewing booked, signed or lost so that I know where every deal stands.
  • As a team member, I want the referral fee calculated when I record a signed contract so that invoicing is accurate.
  • As a team member, I want to see fees owed and fees received so that I can chase unpaid invoices.

Requirements

Must have for launch (P0)

P0-1Housing search and enquiryBuilt

Students can browse active residences, filter by their university, budget and saved, open a residence, and send a question or viewing request.

Acceptance criteria
  • Only residences marked "show to students" appear.
  • The listing card shows the cheapest available room and its billing period; a residence with no available rooms shows "Currently full" and can't take viewing requests.
  • An enquiry can't be sent without a valid phone or WhatsApp number, and a question can't be sent without a message.
  • The form tells the student which details will be shared with the residence before they send.
  • A student can never change an enquiry's status or the money fields.
P0-2Enquiry pipeline and fee trackingBuilt

The Havenly team sees every enquiry, contacts the student and the residence, moves it through the pipeline, and records signed contracts.

Acceptance criteria
  • Marking an enquiry signed requires the contract value; the fee is calculated from that residence's fee % and saved at that moment, so later changes to the % don't alter it.
  • Moving an enquiry off "signed" clears its contract value and fee.
  • Fee terms and partner contacts are only visible to admins.
  • Saving a residence, its rooms and its terms either all succeeds or all fails.
P0-3Lead response within 24 hoursIn-app only

Every new enquiry reaches the team immediately and is passed to the residence the same day.

Acceptance criteria
  • Every admin gets a notification when an enquiry is sent; tapping it opens that enquiry.
  • The student is notified when their enquiry moves to contacted, viewing booked or signed.
  • Until push notifications exist, a named team member checks Manage housing at least twice a day.
P0-4Legal and complianceOpen
  • Company name and privacy contact email filled into the privacy policy and terms.
  • A UAE lawyer has reviewed the policies, the minimum age, and whether the referral-fee model needs a RERA licence.
  • The policies are hosted at a public web address for the app stores.
  • A written partner agreement covering the fee, when it's due, and how a signed student is attributed to Havenly.
P0-5Account deletion keeps records correctOpen
  • When a student deletes their account, signed referral records stay (without their personal details) so the fee can still be invoiced.
  • Their uploaded photos and videos are deleted from storage, not only their database records.
P0-6Store-ready buildsOpen
  • Production iOS and Android builds, tested on real devices, including photo upload and the map.
  • Store listings with screenshots, and data-collection declarations that match the privacy policy.
P0-7Founding residences liveOpen
  • At least [N] residences listed, each with photos, room types and prices, nearby universities, map coordinates and fee terms.

Should have soon after launch (P1)

  • Push notifications for new enquiries and status changes, so leads aren't missed when the app is closed. Needs a development build and an Apple Developer account.
  • Usage measurement. Track Housing tab views and residence page views, so the enquiry rate can be measured. The app has no analytics today.
  • Response-time view in Manage housing: how long each enquiry has waited, with overdue ones highlighted.
  • Residence notifications by email or WhatsApp, so leads reach the residence without the team copying them across.

Later (P2)

  • Residence self-service: their own login to update rooms, prices and availability.
  • Roommate matching for shared rooms, using students' interests.
  • Student reviews of residences after move-in.
  • Automated invoicing of referral fees.
  • Other emirates, starting with Sharjah's university cluster.

Design choices today should not block these: residences, rooms and terms are already separate records, and enquiries already carry a status history.

Risks

  • Brokerage regulation. Taking a fee on Dubai rentals may count as brokerage and need a RERA licence. Mitigation: legal review before the first partner signs; keep Havenly out of contracts and payments.
  • Residences bypassing Havenly. A residence could sign a student we introduced without reporting it. Mitigation: an attribution clause in the partner agreement, the enquiry record as evidence, and asking students to confirm when they sign.
  • Empty housing tab at launch. Students lose trust if there's nothing listed. Mitigation: housing surfaces only appear once residences are live; sign founding partners first.
  • Slow lead handling. Without push notifications, leads can wait overnight. Mitigation: a daily owner for the pipeline until push ships.
  • Data stored outside the UAE. The database is hosted in India. Mitigation: disclosed in the privacy policy; confirm with the lawyer whether this is acceptable.
