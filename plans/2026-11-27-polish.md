# Plan: polish v2 (premium feel, remove leftovers)

## Goal
The app feels premium and consistent with the v2 look (`design/handoff/README.md`), and leftovers from earlier versions are gone. Approved by the owner on 2026-11-25/26.

## Steps
1. Faster launch: `src/components/LaunchAnimation.tsx` becomes a quick fade of the wordmark (about 0.4 s in all), keeping the safety timeout.
2. Remove the daily-streak counter: delete `src/lib/useStreakBump.ts` and its call in `src/navigation/RootNavigator.tsx`. The database function and demo mode's refusal of `bump_streak` stay (the guest test checks that refusal, not the hook, so no test change is needed).
3. Fewer pills on the listing page (`ResidenceDetailScreen.tsx`): only Verified (or Not checked / Sample) and Student deal stay as pills; the kind of place and "Bills included" become plain text lines. Cards keep at most one badge on the photo plus "Bills included".
4. Housing top bar (`HousingScreen.tsx`): only the Saved heart for signed-in users; the "My enquiries" clipboard button goes (My enquiries stays in Profile).
5. Restyle old-look screens to v2 (tokens, 44 px round back button, Schibsted Grotesk 800 titles at 30 px, 22 px card radius, rows like Profile's cards, chevron icons instead of a typed "›"): Settings, Notifications, Inbox, Chat, Edit profile, Change password, Blocked users, Legal, Onboarding. Look only, no behaviour change.
6. Quieter empty photo boxes (`PhotoPlaceholder.tsx`): flat `colors.photo`, no gradient, a small icon.
7. Profile's Account card: remove the "Messages" row (messages live in Community); keep "Saved places".
8. Listing page: one renting-guide link, "Before you pay: read the renting guide", near the cost / enquire area; the other links to the guide on that page go. The Housing link stays.
9. Community tab: remove the "Team" row (Settings already has Review reports, Manage events, Manage perks).
10. `useConfirmDiscard.ts`: "Discard your draft?" works on the web (`window.confirm`), Alert elsewhere.
11. Area tags fill the Housing search: `MainTabParamList.Housing: { area?: string } | undefined`; HousingScreen reads `area` into its search text; the area tag chip on a post navigates to `MainTabs` → `Housing` with `{ area }`.
12. `supabase/schema.sql`: append the sample-listings migration under a "SAMPLE LISTINGS" heading.
