# Plan: the new logo

Branch: `feat/new-logo`. Chief Architect. Chosen by the owner on 2026-11-18: option B, the roof-h icon with the plain wordmark; separate from the tees brand.

## Goal
Replace the old coral, swirly-script logo with the roof-h and the plain wordmark everywhere in the app.

## Steps
1. Make the assets: app icon, Android icon, browser icon, splash wordmark, mark SVG and a logo sheet (`design/brand/`, `assets/`). Android icon background colour in `app.json` is now oxblood.
2. Add `src/components/Brand.tsx` (`RoofH` and `Wordmark`) and the ExtraBold font to `App.tsx`.
3. Use them in the launch animation, Login, Sign up, Feed and Housing, instead of the old dot and italic text.

## Files
`assets/*.png`, `app.json` (adaptive icon colour), `App.tsx`, `src/components/Brand.tsx`, `src/components/LaunchAnimation.tsx`, `src/screens/LoginScreen.tsx`, `SignUpScreen.tsx`, `FeedScreen.tsx`, `HousingScreen.tsx`, `design/brand/`.

## Checks
typecheck, lint, tests, expo-doctor and the web export pass; the launch animation, Housing, Login and the Feed show the new logo.

## What could break
The new font weight must be loaded or the wordmark falls back to a system font (it is loaded in `App.tsx`).

## Not doing
